Terms of Service

Version of 23 November 2026

These are the rules for playing here. Making an account means you agree to them.

1. Your account

  • You must be 13 or older to make an account.
  • One person per account. Keep your password and your memory card code to yourself; whoever has them can play as you.
  • Use an email address you can read. It is only used for your account, such as resetting your password.

2. How to behave

  • No harassment, hate, threats, sexual content, or sharing other people's private details, in text chat, voice chat, or names.
  • No cheating: no edited saves, bots, or tools that change what the server sees.
  • Do not try to break, overload, or get into the server or other players' accounts.

3. Moderation: chat and voice are not private

  • Moderators and admins can read world and room text chat, and the server keeps a short log of recent chat lines.
  • Moderators and admins can listen live to the voice chat of any room, without being seen in that room. Voice is not recorded or stored.
  • Moderators and admins can speak by voice to one player. You see their name unless they are hidden. This is not recorded or stored.
  • Staff can remove chat lines, mute your chat or voice, remove you from the game, or ban your account, for a time or for good, when these rules are broken.
  • What staff do with these tools is logged.

Moderation helps, but nobody can keep everyone safe online. Be careful about what you share, and report or mute players who break these rules.

4. What we keep

  • Your username, your email address, and a scrambled form of your password that cannot be read back.
  • Your Chao and their progress.
  • The last 200 chat lines, held in memory only and gone when the server restarts.
  • A record of any mute, removal, or ban on your account, and the log of what staff did.

We do not sell this to anyone. It sits on a server rented from a hosting company, and your email address is passed to a mail service only to send your password reset mail.

To ask a question, appeal a ban, or have your account and its data deleted, write to us on our Discord. Deletion is done within 30 days of your request.
