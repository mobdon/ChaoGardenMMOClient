Sonic Adventure 2

Recompiled from the GameCube release. The game files come from your own copy and never leave your computer: pick your Steam game folder (steamapps/common/Sonic Adventure 2), recommended, or your SA2 Battle disc. With both, Steam's files are used and the disc only fills gaps. A folder you don't pick is read from this computer's local server.
