# ASOE single-player springs

The normal up/down, left/right and four diagonal objects are map IDs 8–15. The 18 layout slots contain 345 such placements. The two hiding springs use a separate task (ID 108). The normal-gravity Sonic implementation reads animation 455, both native hitbox slots, native palette, flip bits, idle/active variants and source Q8.8 impulses. It emits sound ID 170 independently of audio playback.

The impulse table at 0xB84FC is four signed little-endian halfwords (1920, 2304, 2688, 3072), SHA-256 e0812431e8a7d2d30fadb2beb095370239239532628ce36c9957254214264724. The upward routine reads its pointer at 0x22780. Signed parameter zero >= 4 selects the unsigned second byte shifted left four bits. Negative parameter zero selects the preset indexed by its low two bits. On up/diagonal contact bit zero selects character state 17 or 21. Horizontal launches preserve ground/air status while setting X and ground speed. Downward launches preserve character art.

Animation slot 455 at 0xFEAC4 points to the 32-byte variant table at 0xEAFC4, SHA-256 e4e84667d29c610253e45da28d46d26853b6292e70f7a3eca81460fd61b06fc6. Its eight entry offsets are 0xEAC7C, 0xEACAC, 0xEAD5C, 0xEAD8C, 0xEAE3C, 0xEAE78, 0xEAF28 and 0xEAF64. Even variants are idle. The native constructors flip X for IDs 10, 12 and 14 and Y for ID 9. Normal up with parameter bit one clear uses source palette bank 36 at 0x11ACD4, length 32, SHA-256 37aae7e82b8453470eccba39a87149a7440a7c9fe645275d4b5f6986095469d0. The constructor copies that palette into OBJ bank 8 and increments the object's palette bank by one. The render regression hashes both up palettes and all eight orientations. The tests also activate each kind with its actual native OBJ hitboxes and advance the native animation back to idle.

The independent CPU oracle used Unicorn in ARM Thumb mode and ran all five unmodified contact routines from the fingerprinted ASOE ROM. These entry points sit 0x30 bytes before the SAT-R routine-name offsets in this ROM. The harness mapped the ROM at 0x08000000 and emulated work RAM, IWRAM and I/O. R0–R3 supplied a native-layout sprite, map record, spring and Sonic respectively. The spring anchor was (80,80), the player used body offsets 6/14, camera min/max Y were 0/1024, gravity was normal and game mode was zero. The harness executed the ROM's platform and sideways classifiers. It stubbed Amy hammer contact false, player transition helpers and sprite-offset adjustment as returning, and recorded m4aSongNumStart's R0 argument. The tested actors were already standing height. The unit tests preserve the measured post-call positions, velocities, ground speed, animation variants and sound events. Inputs cover all eight IDs and signed/custom strengths. These are function-level comparisons. No ROM instructions execute in the Rust runtime.

The collision helper covers the launch faces. It omits the generic platform terrain probe that can redirect a side separation around adjacent solid tiles, persistent stood-object bookkeeping, the Amy hammer bonus, inverted-gravity staging, multiplayer and the hiding spring.
