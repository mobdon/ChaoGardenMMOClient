# Seven-ring course finish and special-stage entry boundary

Source: pinned A2NE shared/stage/player.c 0e99fe239ec75a8d188769913aafd9485dc76908 (Player_InitSpecialStageTransition, Player_80279F8, Player_8027B98, sub_80232D0); sa2/ui/stage_results.c 27c7baf8463f161552a37f298f93f0fce099806a. Source commit 2dd3f17d30cd3c156e508aac0017620ae485b9ba. Scope is ordinary campaign Sonic and normal gravity.

At the goal with at least seven rings the player starts the special approach immediately after transition setup. The approach adjusts ground speed relative to horizontal air speed, advances the timer, chooses state 32 at tick 120, and creates ordinary stage results at 180. While results tally, the approach keeps running. The results task hides its graphics and sets move-state 0x04000000 when the special fade begins. On the next player callback, after ground motion, Sonic enters boosted rightward motion at Q(10), requests original boost effects and SE_273, then subsequent callbacks reduce camera.unk8 by 56 Q8 units after each movement. The original lightening fade lasts 128 updates. After level advancement and an in-memory campaign write, the boundary is the original request CreateSpecialStage(-1,-1); this is not playable special-stage gameplay.

During this exit, pre-movement sub_80232D0 wraps the player's horizontal position, camera X and camera target X using the verified source ROM tables at 0xD650C and 0xD661C. Thresholds differ by act: the 14 ordinary acts have -960 pixel displacement except Music Plant 2 (-959). The source has no vertical wrap in those acts. An unused camera.unk20 accumulation is not exposed by the adapter; no Cheese actor exists in this Sonic path. The special-stage scene, its rendering and reward logic are separate.

Tests/native_special_gate_oracle.py compiles the original callbacks with flat motion, instrumented event services and no actual terrain; the Rust outputs match all 20,208 snapshots (48 scenarios × 421 ticks), including exact camera, boost and result events. The horizontal wrap branch also matches all 336 checks (14 tables × 24 subpixel/guard cases). Existing independent results, fade and placement tests cover those separate systems.
