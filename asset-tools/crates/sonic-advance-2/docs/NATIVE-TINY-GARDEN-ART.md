# A2NE cartridge practice frames

The two public functions in native_garden_art accept a VerifiedRom and the current public Memory or Bounce view. They produce a 240 by 160 RGBA8 inspection frame from the embedded English Tiny Chao Garden. No installed PNG, generated atlas, server, or other checkout is read by either renderer.

Memory reconstructs BG3's 6 by 5 zone map from the original 0x15e10 template and composites the BG2 and BG1 sidebar maps. Occupied cells use the cartridge's 32 by 32 OBJ card back or the face currently disclosed by the gameplay view. Empty cells show the board. The renderer draws cards at their grid locations; it does not reproduce intermediate flip matrices, an in-transit card, the special trip animation, result/pause overlays, or changing sidebar digits. The original 16 by 16 selector is drawn at the unpressed settled target of the selected cell (0200A638). In a stepped inspection the selector snaps to the current input cell instead of easing there. For normal walking, the public shuffle view supplies position, direction, mirror and animation counter: the renderer composes the five original OBJ pieces (020099F8, 02009A70) using the native normal-appearance palette. During the trip it omits the walker.

Bounce reconstructs BG3 from the original 24 by 40 map, crops it at the round's vertical scroll, and overlays the static English HUD. It resolves each visible actor's native descriptor/tile/bank, including per-piece anchors, flips and sprite size, with viewport clipping. Clouds, targets, bumpers, spring children and Chao are submitted in that order. Zero-angle affine parts use their signed 8.8 inverse scale around the native canvas center; nonzero-angle or missing matrices are omitted. This is layer inspection: hardware OBJ priority ties, blend/window and fade state, menus, live digits and transformed-frame pixel parity are not established. No substitute sprites or handmade markers are drawn.

The standalone adaptation derives from the client's asset-tools/crates/tiny-game-presentation/src/lib.rs (SHA-256 e8b5a49eb8f00950cfd030175f83873da7d99924dc6b58ea626bf94138942f51). That project's tools/verify_native_art.py compares textures/maps to original Thumb loaders and OAM helpers with functional BIOS graphics shims; proof/native-art.json records the previous pass. The crate test pins its raw RGBA8 fingerprints for the Memory background, seven faces and back, Bounce world and HUD, and all 85 catalogued Bounce actor textures/compositions. It also verifies removal of one Memory card restores exactly its board region and exercises two distinct playable Bounce views. Neither proof asserts a pixel-perfect full hardware frame. No cartridge graphics are stored in source.

Run with A2NE_ROM pointing to the local verified cartridge:
    cargo test --lib native_garden_art -- --ignored

The independent tests/native_memory_art_oracle.py executes the original Thumb cursor positioning for all 30 cells and walker OAM composition for nine tiles in both orientations. It decodes native OAM and RGBA, independently of Rust. All 30 complete transparent selector overlays and 18 complete five-part walker overlays match byte for byte. The retained Garden OBJ bank 1 colors the selector; Memory loads its own banks beginning at bank 2. Reproduce with Python, Unicorn 2.1.4, the verified multiboot, and the client tiny-game-presentation/tools directory as its two arguments.
